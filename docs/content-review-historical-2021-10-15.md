This is a separate **historical practice shift**, not a current travel checklist or a replacement for the modern daily series. The authored flight leaves DEL on **2021-10-15 at 12:00 IST (UTC+05:30), or 06:30 UTC**. Its reference snapshot applies to that exact travel date. Historical applicability and the present source-review date are separate fields.

## Dated primary evidence

- [Transport Canada, 21 September 2021](https://www.canada.ca/en/transport-canada/news/2021/09/canada-announces-extension-of-flight-ban-from-india-as-it-prepares-for-the-return-of-direct-flights.html): direct flights could resume after the restriction expired on 26 September. The additional requirement was a negative molecular result from the approved Genestrings laboratory at Delhi airport, collected within 18 hours of scheduled direct departure. Eligible fully vaccinated passengers still needed their test and ArriveCAN information. Indirect routes had a different third-country testing requirement, outside this shift.
- [Public Safety Canada / CBSA border-measures timeline, received 9 November 2021](https://search.open.canada.ca/qpnotes/record/ps-sp%2CTo%20be%20added): retrospectively confirms the 27 September resumption and the designated-lab/18-hour measures. Its received date is not a legal effective date or a page-update date.
- [Transport Canada, 28 January 2022](https://www.canada.ca/en/transport-canada/news/2022/01/government-of-canada-provides-update-about-canadas-border-measures.html): explicitly removes the modified India/Morocco pre-departure requirements on that date. This later publication establishes the end of the historical regime, rather than supplying modern rules to an earlier date. The snapshot source coverage ends on 2022-01-27; the authored cases are pinned more narrowly to 2021-10-15.
- [Air Canada’s retained 2021 India-flight notice](https://www.aircanada.com/in/en/aco/home/book/travel-news-and-updates/2021/flights-from-india.html): corroborates the use of RT-PCR and rapid PCR at the designated airport location. Its carrier-specific processing lead times are **not** adopted as universal government or fictional-airline boarding requirements. The URL identifies the 2021 notice, but no exact publication date is inferred.

The in-game source summaries use the first two government publications. Later removal and carrier corroboration are recorded here for the temporal audit. Publication metadata, verification date and effective dates are not interchangeable.

## Closed scope

Every passenger is an adult Canadian citizen with a genuine current Canadian passport and no additional permission needed for return. Vaccination, ArriveCAN, identity matching, symptoms, masks, quarantine arrangements and unrelated carrier conditions are stipulated satisfied. No prior-positive recovery report, medical exception, age exemption or other test exemption applies. This avoids silently treating every traveller as subject to the same rule in a much broader immigration/public-health model.

The puzzle isolates direct DEL–YVR pre-departure testing. It does not model connecting journeys, third-country tests, recovery windows, border admission, quarantine assessment or arbitrary historical date selection. Those require separate dated sources and case design. The 72-hour general rule is a deliberate distractor; it is not the authored direct-route limit.

Air Beluga's procedure for requesting replacement evidence and referring unresolved conflicts is explicitly **fictional carrier training policy**. Government sources establish the underlying document requirement, not a universal escalation mandate. A confirmed unmet requirement after alternatives are exhausted differs from a material fact the available checks cannot establish.

## Temporal implementation and verification

`COVID_TEST` reports carry a collection instant (or an explicitly unresolved printed time), report instant, method, result, laboratory and location. They do not invent a calendar expiry date. Offsets are preserved on the card. The itinerary exposes the exact scheduled departure used for comparison. A later report issue time never resets specimen age.

Validation checks hour limits using UTC instants, including the exact 18-hour boundary, and rejects future collection, late reporting, wrong methods, wrong or unresolved providers, missing offsets and incomplete check-in paths. Health validation uses the selected snapshot and flight time, never today's clock. Historical stages must identify themselves and match their pinned snapshot date. Sources may be reviewed today without pretending they were verified in 2021.
