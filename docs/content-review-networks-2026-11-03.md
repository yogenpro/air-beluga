## Government sources and limits

All sources below were read on 2026-11-03 UTC. Page publication dates are not inferred effective dates. Current snapshots cover this authored date only; future daily selection dates do not move the travel date.

- Japan MOFA: [short-stay exemptions and passport conditions](https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/short/novisa.html), including Thai electronic passports and Indonesian pre-registration; [JAPAN eVISA](https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/visaonline.html), including the live issuance-notice requirement. Cases stipulate qualifying U.S./UK residence and ordinary passports when using eVISA.
- Japanese government: [Palau embassy visa guidance](https://www.palau.emb-japan.go.jp/itpr_en/visa_info.html) distinguishes immediate airside connections from airport changes and other border crossings. Its August 2026 update also identifies the discontinuation of the separate transit visa category from 1 July 2026: entry permission for transit purposes now uses the temporary visitor route. Older Portland/Seattle PDF search results returned 404; Anchorage's March guidance predates that change, so none is used as the current rule source. No overnight Narita airside assumption is made.
- Hong Kong Immigration Department: [Indian pre-arrival registration](https://www.immd.gov.hk/eng/services/visas/pre-arrival_registration_for_indian_nationals.html), its airside and diplomatic/official-passport exceptions and passport linkage. [General visit/transit arrangements](https://www.immd.gov.hk/eng/services/visas/visit_transit.html) cover the alternative of issued permission. Ordinary-passport cases explicitly exclude unmentioned residence, e-Channel, crew and other exemptions. Registration slips are modeled as electronic evidence with an explicit printed-slip assumption where used for entry; this does not change the official requirement.
- Singapore ICA: [transit definition](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/transiting-singapore), [visa requirements and arrival-card distinction](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore/visa_requirements), [96-hour VFTF and qualifying passes](https://www.ica.gov.sg/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/entry-requirements/visa-free-transit-facility). [Singapore Embassy in Paris](https://paris.mfa.gov.sg/consular-services/visa-information/) confirms passport validity measured from arrival. No claim of guaranteed border admission is made.

Separate tickets alone never establish a border crossing: the authored baggage and boarding-pass facts determine that. An exhausted or short-validity pass is not silently assumed sufficient. Used single-journey visa return exceptions are deliberately excluded by explicit case facts (unused permission, onward Japan trip, no qualifying direct return to India). Yellow-fever history, arrival-card submission where required, funds, return/onward arrangements and unrelated checks are stipulated satisfied except where expressly tested.

Unsupported combinations excluded from this finite scope: recognition of refugee/emergency documents in new jurisdictions, mainland-China/Taiwan/HK special entry arrangements, additional citizenship, work/study entry, long stays, overnight Narita transfers, visas on arrival, unverified hub routes, general immigration discretion and live operational data. Existing Canadian and historical stages retain their published evidence and answers.
